Besan Ladoo Recipe (Traditional Homemade Style)

Besan Ladoo is a popular Indian sweet made with gram flour (besan), ghee, sugar, and dry fruits. It is soft, aromatic, and perfect for festivals like Ganesh Chaturthi, Diwali, and Raksha Bandhan.

Ingredients (Makes 15–18 Ladoos)

IngredientQuantity
Besan (Gram Flour)2 cups
Ghee¾ cup
Powdered Sugar1 cup
Cardamom Powder½ teaspoon
Chopped Almonds2 tablespoons
Chopped Pistachios2 tablespoons
Raisins (Optional)1 tablespoon

Step 1: Roast the Besan

Heat a heavy pan and add ghee.

  • Add the besan to the melted ghee.

  • Roast on low flame for 15–20 minutes.

  • Stir continuously until it becomes golden and releases a nutty aroma.

Tip: Do not roast on high heat; it may burn.

Step 2: Add Dry Fruits

  • Add chopped almonds, pistachios, and raisins.

  • Roast for another 2 minutes.

Step 3: Cool the Mixture

  • Remove from the stove.

  • Let the mixture cool for 8–10 minutes.

  • It should be warm, not hot.

Step 4: Mix Sugar & Cardamom

  • Add powdered sugar.

  • Add cardamom powder.

  • Mix everything well until smooth.

Step 5: Shape the Ladoos

  • Take a small portion in your palm.

  • Press and roll into a round ladoo.

  • Garnish with pistachio or almond pieces.

Ready Besan Ladoo

Cooking Time

StepTime
Preparation10 minutes
Roasting20 minutes
Cooling10 minutes
Making Ladoos10 minutes

Total Time

50 minutes

Tips for Perfect Besan Ladoo

  • Roast besan on low flame for the best taste.

  • Use fresh desi ghee for rich flavor.

  • Add sugar only after the mixture cools slightly.

  • Store in an airtight container for 2–3 weeks.
